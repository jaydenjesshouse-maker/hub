# Our Hub

Jayden and Jess's shared hub: money, calendar and notes.

- `index.html` is the page on your Home Screen. It gives the hub its icon, opens it full screen, and remembers the passcode. The hub itself is loaded from Google Apps Script.
- `manifest.json`, `icon-180.png` and `icon-512.png` are the icon and app details.

Nothing in this repository contains any figures, notes or events. Those stay in a private Google Sheet and Google Calendar.
