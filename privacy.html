Privacy

Our Hub is a private app used by two people, Jayden and Jess. It isn't offered to anyone else.

What the hub reads

When you connect a card or account through open banking (via Enable Banking), the hub reads only what you approve:

  • the account or card name
  • the balance and available credit
  • transactions: date, amount, merchant name and category

It can't move money, make payments or change anything with the bank. Access is read-only.

Where it's kept

  • In a private Google Sheet and Google Apps Script project owned by Jayden. Nothing is kept on any other server.
  • Transactions are kept for about 120 days, then removed automatically.
  • The keys that let the hub talk to Enable Banking are kept in the script's private settings, never in the app or on GitHub.

Who can see it

  • Each person's cards and payments are shown only on their own phone, behind the hub's passcode.
  • The other person only sees that you have cards, and how many.
  • Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising. No analytics or tracking are used.

Who else is involved

  • Enable Banking Oy connects to the bank or card provider and passes the data on.
  • Google hosts the Sheet and the script.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's front page, which holds no personal data.

Stopping it

  • Open banking access ends by itself after 90 days unless you approve it again.
  • In the hub, Forget this card ends the connection and deletes that card's stored data straight away.
  • You can also withdraw access at any time in your bank's or card provider's app.

Questions

Ask Jayden, who runs the hub. Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete anything it holds about you.
